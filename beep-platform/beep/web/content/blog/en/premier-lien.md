# A link that stays useful after sharing

A short link makes a destination easier to share on WhatsApp, a poster or a business card. Its real value appears when the destination needs to change.

## Keep the link, change the destination

Create your link in your account. When your campaign page changes, edit the destination: the shared address and dynamic QR codes that follow it stay the same.

## Check before publishing

Open the link on your phone and computer. For mobile apps, the simulator helps compare visitor profiles; testing on a real device is still necessary.

## Measure what helps you decide

Review clicks and scans, then compare devices and referrers. An unknown country may simply mean the geolocation database is not configured.
